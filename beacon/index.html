Every email read. Every order up to date.

Beacon is the AI operations hub behind an operations team’s orders. Every 15 minutes it reads every email in the operations mailbox, updates orders, shipments and requests, and prices quotations and prepares Xero invoice drafts. It never sends email: client replies wait as drafts, and anything it isn’t sure about goes to a person.

Engineering notes
  • Nothing missed, nothing doubled. Each run re-scans a 6-hour overlap and skips mail already processed.
  • Sent mail reads differently. What we send is a confirmed action; what a supplier sends is a claim.
  • Attachments are evidence. PDFs, spreadsheets and photos are read; order refs come from file names too.
  • Doubts go to a person. If the second check disagrees, the change waits in Needs review. Every Keep or Discard is saved to measure accuracy.
  • Only live facts. It reads the email, the thread and live project data, so old order details can't leak into new updates.
  • Failures don't block the queue. Retries with backoff; 3 failures and a person takes it.
  • Runs on free tiers. Big price lists (the largest has 240k lines) are split into sorted chunks with a binary-search index.
  • Tested before it ships. Automated suites cover the API, permissions, screens and invoice builder.
