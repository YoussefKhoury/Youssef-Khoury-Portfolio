# Service Request Workflow — Requirements & UAT Case

Business-analysis portfolio case for a service-request process, intentionally different from the order-to-delivery analytics case.

## Business problem

Requests arrive with inconsistent fields, unclear ownership and unreliable SLA handling. The proposed workflow governs intake, routing, escalation, closure and reporting.

## Deliverables

- 24 approved or review-ready functional, reporting and non-functional requirements.
- User stories and testable acceptance criteria.
- Requirements traceability matrix with full UAT coverage.
- 30 UAT cases with pass, fail and blocked evidence.
- RAID log, executive readiness summary and service-request baseline.
- Formula-driven Excel workbook linking requirements, tests, risks and KPIs.

## Decision use

The pack gives stakeholders one audit trail from business need to requirement, acceptance criteria, test evidence, defect and go-live decision.
