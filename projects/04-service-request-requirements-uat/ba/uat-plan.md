# UAT Plan

## Objective

Verify that the proposed service-request workflow supports controlled intake, routing, SLA management, escalation, closure, reporting, security and auditability.

## Entry criteria

- Requirements approved or accepted for test.
- Test roles and synthetic records available.
- Priority, SLA and routing rules configured.
- Defect severity and retest ownership agreed.

## Exit criteria

- Every must-have requirement has a linked test.
- No open critical defect.
- Failed cases have an owner and retest date.
- Blocked cases have a documented dependency.
- KPI results reconcile to the request register.

## Evidence

The workbook records requirement ID, scenario, result, evidence and tester for each of 30 UAT cases.
